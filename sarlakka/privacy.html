Privacy Policy

Sarlakka: Likhit & Trial · by Vexel Interactive
Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information Sarlakka uses and why. The short version: there is no account and no server of ours; your study progress is saved on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure them.

Not a government service. Sarlakka is an unofficial study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Transport Management (DoTM) or the Government of Nepal, and it does not collect anything on their behalf.

Who we are

Sarlakka is made by Vexel Interactive. You can reach us at rosanstha55@gmail.com.

No account needed

You don't create an account or sign in. We don't ask for your email address, phone number, contacts or licence number. A name you type on your practice card stays on your device.

Your progress stays on your device

Your answers, mock scores, practice cards, language and settings are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup. You can delete it at any time in the app (Me → Progress & data → Reset progress) or by uninstalling the app.

What the app sends

Sarlakka has no servers of its own, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not send your progress, your answers or any licence details anywhere. The only network traffic comes from Google's advertising service, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sarlakka shows full-screen ads at natural breaks, after you finish a practice session (from the second session on, at most three a day). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • diagnostics and information about how you interact with the ads.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Test versions of the app show Google's test ads only.

Your choices about ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

Reporting a mistake and sharing

"Report a mistake" opens your own email app with a draft; nothing is sent unless you send it. Sharing a result or practice card uses Android's share menu and only goes where you choose to send it.

Purchases

This version has no purchases.

Children

Sarlakka is meant for people preparing for a driving licence. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If how the app uses information changes, we will update this page and the date above.
