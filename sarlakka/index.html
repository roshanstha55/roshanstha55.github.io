Sarlakka (सर्लक्क, "smoothly, without a hitch") helps you prepare for the written exam (Likhit) and the practical trial for a Nepali driving licence.

  • Likhit questions for category A/K (motorcycle, scooter) and category B (car, jeep, delivery van), 500 each
  • Mock exam in the published format: 25 questions, 30 minutes, pass at 60
  • Practice by topic with instant feedback, and a weak-area review
  • Traffic signs with flashcards
  • 3D trial practice: a bike for A/K, a car for B, with a track guide for every part
  • Nepali first, with English; no account; study works offline

Where the questions come from

The questions are taken from the Likhit question collections published by the Department of Transport Management as PDF files: the Category B (car, jeep, delivery van) question collection, revised FY 2082/83 (वर्ग ख / B … लिखित परीक्षाको वस्तुगत प्रश्नोत्तर सङ्ग्रह, परिमार्जन आ.व. २०८२/२०८३), and the Category A/K (motorcycle, scooter, moped) collection of the same revision. The app copies them as published and may contain typing slips; each question has a "Report a mistake" button.

The English text is an unofficial translation made for this app. The Nepali text is the published wording.

Trial layouts and some trial point values in the app are approximate and are labelled "approximate — check at your transport office". Always confirm the current rules with your transport office and dotm.gov.np before your exam.
