Glowmere

Logic puzzles by Vexel Interactive · Light the way home.

The Hush has scattered the Glowlings of Glowmere. Help Pip, a small seed spirit, guide each one back to the one spot that is truly theirs.

The rules fit in three lines:

  • Every coloured patch gets exactly one light.
  • Every row and every column gets exactly one light.
  • Lights never touch — not even corners.

300 hand-checked levels (every one has exactly one solution), a new Moonrise puzzle every night, story chapters to unlock, no timers, and it plays fully offline.
