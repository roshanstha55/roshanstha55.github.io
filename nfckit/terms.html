1. The app

NFC Kit lets you read, write, copy, erase and inspect NFC tags, and write task and check-in tags that run actions on your phone. Version 1.0 is free, has no ads and no in-app purchases, and works offline. By installing or using NFC Kit you agree to these terms.

2. Your tags and your data

  • Everything you create (tags written, saved tags, check-ins, history, places) belongs to you and stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
  • You are responsible for what you write to a tag and where you place it. Anyone with an NFC phone can read a tag, so don't write passwords or private information you wouldn't want others to see. A Wi-Fi tag shares that network's password with whoever taps it.
  • Only write to tags you own or are allowed to change. Erasing, formatting or overwriting a tag permanently replaces what was on it.

3. Task tags, check-ins and reminders

  • Task tags run the steps you choose when the tag is tapped with your phone unlocked. Android, your phone maker or other apps may limit, delay or block some steps; NFC Kit tells you when a step didn't run.
  • Check-in reminders are delivered by Android and may arrive a few minutes late, or not at all if notifications are turned off or the phone is in a power-saving mode. Do not rely on NFC Kit for anything where a missed or late reminder could cause harm, such as medical treatment or safety-critical tasks.
  • "Remember where I parked" saves an approximate position from your phone's location services. It can be inaccurate, especially indoors or underground.
