NFC Kit

Tag reader & writer by Vexel Interactive · Tap a tag, get it done.

NFC Kit turns cheap NFC stickers into one-tap shortcuts. Write a tag, stick it where you need it, and tap your phone to it.

Free, no ads, no account, and it works completely offline. Android only.

Task tags

Stick a tag by your bed, desk or car door and tap it to run up to two steps:

  • Set an alarm or start a timer, add a calendar event
  • Open an app, a link or a playlist
  • Navigate home or to work, or remember where you parked and walk back to your car
  • Call, text or email someone
  • Set media or alarm volume, flashlight, vibrate, show a message, copy text

Tap again to undo: the second tap puts the flashlight and volume back the way they were, with more settings coming. Add a condition so a tag only acts at certain times or days, while charging or with headphones in.

Check-in tags

Put a tag on the pet food, the plant pot or your gym bag. Each tap logs it as done, with streaks, history and a 7-day chart. Start/stop time tracking shows hours per day, and an optional reminder nudges you if it hasn't been tapped by a set time.

Everyday tags and tools

Write Wi-Fi, contact, link, text, call, message, place and app tags that work on most NFC phones, even without the app. Read any tag, and erase, format, copy or inspect it byte by byte. Save up to 5 tags and see your last 20 reads and writes.

Privacy

NFC Kit has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no account. Everything you create stays on your phone. Location is read only when you tap a "Remember where I parked" tag. Read the Privacy Policy.
