Privacy Policy

App: NFC Kit: Tag Reader & Writer (com.vexelinteractive.nfckit) · Developer: Vexel Interactive, Australia · Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0

In short

  • NFC Kit collects no personal data and shares nothing.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. Nothing it stores can be sent anywhere by the app.
  • Everything you create stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • NFC tag data: when you read or write a tag, its contents (a link, contact card, Wi-Fi details, text, a task or a check-in) are handled on your phone so they can be shown to you or written to the tag.
  • My Tags and history: tags you save and a history of recent reads, writes and task runs are stored in the app's private storage. You can delete them in My Tags at any time.
  • Check-ins and reminders: the times you tap a check-in tag, your streaks and any reminder time are stored on your phone. Check-in tags themselves only hold a name and an ID, never your taps.
  • Tap again to undo: for undo tasks, the setting the first tap changed (for example the flashlight) is kept on your phone until the second tap puts it back.
  • Places: Home and Work addresses you type in Settings › Places stay on your phone and are never written to tags.
  • Android backup: if you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include NFC Kit's data in your own Google account backup and restore it on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your account; the developer can't see it. You can turn it off in Android settings.

Location

Location is used only by the optional "Remember where I parked" step. NFC Kit asks for permission when you add that step, and reads your location only at the moment you tap that parking tag. The spot is saved on your phone only. There is no background location access, and the location is never sent anywhere. Opening directions hands the address to your maps app.

Other phone features

  • Notifications: asked for only when you first switch on a check-in reminder, and used only for that reminder.
  • Alarms, calendar and other apps: task steps such as "Set an alarm", "Add a calendar event", "Open an app" or "Open a link" hand the request to the app you choose on your phone. NFC Kit cannot read your alarms, calendar or other apps' data. That app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Installed apps: when you pick an app for a tag, NFC Kit lists the launchable apps on your phone. The list is shown on screen only and is never stored or shared.
  • Clipboard: used only when you tap Copy or a task includes a "Copy text" step.
  • Rating: NFC Kit may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Whether you rate, and what you write, is handled by Google Play.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account and no server, there is nothing for the developer to delete. To remove everything NFC Kit stores, delete items in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Data written to a physical NFC tag stays on that tag until you erase it (Tools › Erase tag).

Children

NFC Kit is a general utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes
