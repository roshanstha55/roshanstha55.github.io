Privacy Policy

Skypour: Water Sort Puzzle · by Vexel Interactive
Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information Skypour uses and why. The short version: we don't have our own accounts or servers, and your game progress is saved on your phone. Ads are provided by Google, and Google collects some information to show and measure them. If you allow it, the game also sends Google anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase), so we can fix crashes and make levels fair.

Who we are

Skypour is made by Vexel Interactive. You can reach us at rosanstha55@gmail.com.

No account needed

You don't create an account to play, and everything in the game works without signing in. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, coins, helpers and settings are saved on your device. We (Vexel Interactive) don't receive any of it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, that progress is deleted.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase, optional)

To improve the game we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. When allowed, the game sends Google:

  • Game events – for example "level 57 started / won (2 minutes)", "a helper was used", "a rewarded video was watched", "the tutorial was finished". Only numbers and fixed labels, never free text.
  • Two general properties – roughly how far you've got (a range like "21–60") and whether you bought Remove ads.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics – what went wrong, the device model and Android version, app version.
  • An app-instance identifier created by Firebase. Firebase Analytics in Skypour does not collect your advertising ID.

Where the law requires consent (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), this follows your answer in Google's consent message; elsewhere it's on by default. You can turn it off at any time: Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Remove ads (optional purchase)

You can buy Remove ads once through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game only learns from Google Play whether you own Remove ads, so it can turn the ads off (also after reinstalling). Rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Skypour is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK runs inside the app and, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, it automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers – such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • IP address – which Google uses to estimate your general (approximate) location. We never ask for precise location.
  • Ad interactions – for example app launches, taps on ads and video views.
  • Diagnostic information – such as app launch time and performance.
  • Basic device information – such as the device model and Android version.

This data is sent encrypted (TLS). Google's use of it is described in Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings.

Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad choices.

Rewarded videos are always optional: you only see one if you choose to watch it.

Delete your data

Skypour has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. To delete your data:

  1. Game progress (levels, coins, helpers, settings): uninstall Skypour, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Skypour → Storage → Clear data. This deletes it at once. We never had a copy.
  2. Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase): first turn them off in the game: Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Then email us at rosanstha55@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Skypour data". We will ask Google to delete the Firebase data linked to your device and reply within 30 days. Even without a request, Google deletes this data on its own after a limited time (Firebase Analytics keeps it for up to 14 months, crash reports for 90 days).
  3. Advertising data (Google AdMob): we don't receive or keep it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage what Google keeps in your Google Account.

We don't keep any other data about you. Emails you send us are deleted once your request is answered.

Contact support

"Contact support" in Settings opens your own email app with our address. We only use what you send us to answer you.

Children

Skypour is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their information, contact us and we'll help.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't have our own analytics, tracking or game servers (we use Google's Firebase only as described above).
  • We don't access your photos, files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
