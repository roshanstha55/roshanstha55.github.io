Skypour

Water sort puzzle by Vexel Interactive · Pour the colours back into the sky.

One stormy night the sky's paint spilled into a jumble of bottles and the sky went grey. Help Nimbi, Hoot and Fen sort every colour back into its own bottle and pour it into the sky jars, and watch each floating island light up again.

How to play

  1. Tap a bottle to lift it, then tap another to pour.
  2. You can only pour onto the same colour, or into an empty bottle.
  3. Fill each bottle with one colour. Full bottles fly into their jar.
  4. Stuck? Use Undo, Shuffle or Add bottle. There are no timers and no lives.
