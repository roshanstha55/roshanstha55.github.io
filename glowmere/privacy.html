Privacy Policy

Glowmere: Logic Puzzles · by Vexel Interactive
Last updated: 21 October 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information Glowmere uses and why. The short version: we don't have accounts, we don't run our own servers, and your game progress stays on your phone. Ads in the game are provided by Google, and Google collects some information to show and measure them.

Who we are

Glowmere is made by Vexel Interactive. You can reach us at roshanstha55@gmail.com.

No account, no sign-in

You don't create an account to play. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, score, streak, settings and the nickname you choose are saved only on your device. We don't upload them anywhere. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, that progress is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glowmere is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK runs inside the app and, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, it automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers – such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • IP address – which Google uses to estimate your general (approximate) location. We never ask for precise location.
  • Ad interactions – for example app launches, taps on ads and video views.
  • Diagnostic information – such as app launch time and performance.
  • Basic device information – such as the device model and Android version.

This data is sent encrypted (TLS). Google's use of it is described in Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Rewarded videos are always optional: you only see one if you choose to watch it.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on Moonrise reminders, the game schedules notifications on your phone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to a server for this. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Children

Glowmere is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their information, contact us and we'll help.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't have our own analytics or tracking servers.
  • We don't access your photos, files, contacts, camera or microphone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
