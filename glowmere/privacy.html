Privacy Policy

Glowmere: Logic Puzzles · by Vexel Interactive
Last updated: [date v1.1 is released]

This page explains, in plain English, what information Glowmere uses and why. The short version: we don't have our own accounts or servers, and your game progress is saved on your phone. If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google also keeps a copy of your progress, your achievements and your leaderboard scores in your Google account. Ads are provided by Google, and Google collects some information to show and measure them. If you allow it, the game also sends Google anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase), so we can see which levels are too hard and fix crashes.

Who we are

Glowmere is made by Vexel Interactive. You can reach us at roshanstha55@gmail.com.

No account needed

You don't create an account to play, and everything in the game works without signing in. We don't ask for your email address, phone number or contacts. The name Pip asks for is just a nickname for the game; it stays on your device.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels, stars, score, streak, settings and the nickname you choose are saved on your device. We (Vexel Interactive) don't receive any of it. If you don't use Google Play Games and you uninstall the game or clear its data, that progress is deleted.

Google Play Games (optional)

You can connect the game to Google Play Games (Settings → Google Play Games, or when the game offers it once after level 10). It's entirely optional and the game never waits for it. If you connect:

  • Sign-in – you sign in with your Google account through Google's own screen. Glowmere receives your Play Games profile (your gamer name and a player ID); we never see your password or email address.
  • Cloud save – a copy of your game progress (levels and stars, best scores, story memories, streak, Moonrise results, helpers, star chests and achievements progress) is stored by Google as a Play Games "saved game" in your Google account, so it can come back on a new phone. Your settings and the nickname stay on the device.
  • Achievements and leaderboards – the achievements you earn and your scores for Total Stars, Weekly Moonrise and Moonrise Wins are sent to Google Play Games. Leaderboards can show your gamer name and score to other players, according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. To stop: turn off or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings, or remove Glowmere from "Connected apps" in your Google Account. To delete the cloud copy of your progress, use your Google Account's settings for Play Games / connected app data (or contact us and we'll walk you through it). Uninstalling the game does not delete the copy Google keeps.

Sharing (optional)

After a Moonrise you can tap Share, and after a level Challenge a friend. The game then opens your phone's share menu with a short text (for example your Moonrise number, stars and streak, or a level number and your score, plus a link) and, for Moonrise, a small picture. A challenge link also contains the nickname you gave the game, if you set one. It's only sent to the app and person you choose; nothing is sent to us. When you open a friend's challenge link, the level, score and nickname in it are read by the game on your phone only.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase, optional)

To improve the game we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. When allowed, the game sends Google:

  • Game events – for example "level 57 started / won (3 stars, 2 minutes)", "a hint was used", "a rewarded video was watched", "the tutorial was finished". Only numbers and fixed labels: never your nickname, never a friend's name, never free text.
  • Two general properties – roughly how far you've got (a range like "21–60") and whether you bought Remove ads.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics – what went wrong, the device model and Android version, app version.
  • An app-instance identifier created by Firebase. Firebase Analytics in Glowmere does not collect your advertising ID.

Where the law requires consent (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), this follows your answer in Google's consent message; elsewhere it's on by default. You can turn it off at any time: Settings → Privacy & data → Share anonymous usage data. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and keeps Analytics data for a limited time (see Google Analytics data retention).

Remove ads (optional purchase)

You can buy Remove ads once through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game only learns from Google Play whether you own Remove ads, so it can turn the ads off (also after reinstalling, and on a new phone through Google Play). Rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glowmere is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK runs inside the app and, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, it automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers – such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • IP address – which Google uses to estimate your general (approximate) location. We never ask for precise location.
  • Ad interactions – for example app launches, taps on ads and video views.
  • Diagnostic information – such as app launch time and performance.
  • Basic device information – such as the device model and Android version.

This data is sent encrypted (TLS). Google's use of it is described in Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Rewarded videos are always optional: you only see one if you choose to watch it.

Reminders (optional)

Reminders are off unless you turn them on. If you do, the game schedules them on your phone itself – at most one a day, never between 9 pm and 9 am, and fewer if you haven't opened the game for a while. To pick a good time it remembers, on your phone only, at which hours you usually start a puzzle. Nothing about reminders is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off, or change their style and time, in Settings → Notifications, or in your phone's notification settings.

Event & update news (optional)

If you turn on Event & update news in Settings → Notifications, the game uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send an occasional message about events and updates. For this, Firebase creates a messaging token – a random identifier for the app on your device – and Google uses it to deliver the messages. We send messages only to everyone who turned news on (by "topic": all players, English, app version); we never see your token or send messages to you personally. The game doesn't create a token until you turn news on; turning it off removes the app from the topics and deletes the token. Google processes the token as described in its Firebase privacy information.

Contact support

"Contact support" in Settings opens your own email app with our address. The draft already contains the game's version and your phone's model and Android version, so we can help faster – you can delete them before sending. We only use what you send us to answer you.

Children

Glowmere is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their information, contact us and we'll help.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't have our own analytics, tracking or game servers (we use Google's Firebase only as described above).
  • We don't access your photos, files, contacts, camera or microphone.

Changes
