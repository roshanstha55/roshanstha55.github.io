Glowmere FAQ

What is Glowmere?

Glowmere is a calm logic puzzle game for Android. Each board is split into colour patches, and you place one light in every patch, row and column so that no two lights touch. Along the way you guide the lost Glowlings home through story chapters.

Is Glowmere free?

Yes. Glowmere is free to download and play, with ads. Every level can be played without paying. There is one optional purchase, Remove ads.

Does Glowmere work offline?

Yes. All 300 levels, the hints and your progress work without an internet connection. You only need a connection for ads, optional cloud save through Google Play Games and the optional event news.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes. Moonrise is a new puzzle every night, from level 21. Moonrise boards are Medium or Hard, and if you miss a night you can still play the last seven nights’ puzzles.

How many levels are there, and how hard are they?

There are 300 levels, growing from 4×4 to 9×9 boards. Every level has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing. Easy, Medium and Hard levels are mixed, with a gentler level after each Hard one.

When do ads appear?

Never in the middle of a puzzle and not before level 6. After that, a short break can appear between levels, at most once every three levels. Reward videos (for an extra hint or to refill your fireflies) only play when you choose to watch one.

What does Remove ads do?

Remove ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It turns off the ads between levels for good, also after reinstalling. Reward videos stay available if you want extra hints. Settings → Privacy & about → Restore purchases brings it back on a new phone.

Can I keep my progress on a new phone?

Yes, if you connect Google Play Games (optional, in Settings). Your levels, stars, streak and story progress are then saved in your Google account and come back when you sign in on another phone. Without it, progress is saved only on your phone.

What data does Glowmere collect?

You don’t need an account, and your progress stays on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help us fix problems; you can switch them off at any time in Settings (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they follow your answer to Google’s consent message). The full details are in the privacy policy.

How is Glowmere different from Sudoku and other logic puzzles?

There are no numbers and no arithmetic. You reason about colour patches, rows and columns, and the “no touching” rule. It belongs to the same family as Star Battle puzzles, with boards built so that each one has a single answer you can reach step by step. If you are new to it, read how to solve Glowmere puzzles.

Is Glowmere on iPhone?

Not yet. Glowmere is available for Android on Google Play.
